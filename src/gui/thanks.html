Bitarr is a custom application based on qBittorrent. It would not exist without the qBittorrent project, its maintainers and its many contributors, whose work is the foundation of this program.

Bitarr also builds on libtorrent-rasterbar, Qt, Boost, OpenSSL and zlib. Thanks to everyone who makes and maintains them.

The original qBittorrent authors and contributors are credited on the Authors tab and in the AUTHORS file. Bitarr changes are by BoKu.
